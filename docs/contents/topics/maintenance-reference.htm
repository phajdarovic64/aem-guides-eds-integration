Load and stability limitations

Caution: Do not exceed a distributed accessory payload of 70 kg, excluding the supplied desktop. Uneven or point-loaded weight may reduce stability and performance.

Preventive maintenance schedule

Frequency
Inspection or care
Acceptance criterion
Weekly
Clean desktop with a slightly damp microfiber cloth, then dry.
No standing moisture or abrasive residue
Monthly
Check cable routing, motor connections, and available travel clearance.
Cables remain seated and free from abrasion or pinch points
Every 3 months
Inspect frame and foot fasteners for looseness.
Fasteners are secure and the desk does not rock
Every 6 months
Run one complete unobstructed height cycle and check for unexpected noise.
Both legs move synchronously without abnormal noise
Warning: Disconnect the desk from the wall outlet before assembling, cleaning, inspecting, or servicing electrical or moving components. Do not open the motor housings or power supply.
Clean laminate surfaces with mild soap diluted in water. Never use solvents, abrasive pads, or spray cleaners directly on the handset.
If inspection reveals a damaged lifting-column cable or power supply, stop operating the desk and replace the affected module through an authorized service channel. Do not attempt internal electrical repairs.

Controller fault-code reference

These example codes are defined solely for the fictional NordForm controller in this demonstration.
Code
Possible cause
Recommended action
RST
Calibration required after the first installation or power interruption
Repeat the controller initialization described in assembly and commissioning.
E01
Movement obstruction or collision detection
Remove the obstruction, inspect cable slack, then command a short movement.
E03
Motor synchronization fault
Stop operation, unplug the desk, inspect the M1 and M2 connections, and retry initialization. Contact service if the fault recurs.
HOT
Duty-cycle limit reached
Stop movement and allow at least 18 minutes of rest before another adjustment.
Important: Do not bypass collision sensing or reset repeatedly when a physical obstruction, damaged cable, or unstable leg is present.

Storage and operating conditions

For the illustrative European configuration, connect the supplied power adapter only to a grounded 220-240 V AC outlet.
Keep the desk indoors, away from direct water exposure and heating equipment. Store it in a dry area, with the tabletop protected from impact. Disconnect power before long-term storage.
For normal use and ergonomic positioning, see the product overview.
