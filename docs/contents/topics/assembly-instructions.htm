Assemble and commission the Elevate Pro 160 desk

Assemble the frame and tabletop, connect the electrical components, and perform the first-motion and memory-preset checks.

PREREQUISITE
Allow approximately 45 minutes. Use two adults for handling and turning the desk. Prepare a clean, padded floor area, a 4 mm hex key, and a manual Phillips screwdriver. Verify that all components are present and undamaged.
Warning: Disconnect the desk from the wall outlet before assembling, cleaning, inspecting, or servicing electrical or moving components. Do not open the motor housings or power supply.
  • A: 1600 x 800 mm desktop, quantity 1
  • B: motorized lifting columns, left and right, quantity 2
  • C: adjustable steel crossbar and controller bracket, quantity 1
  • D: floor feet, quantity 2
  • E: memory handset, quantity 1
  • F: control unit and power supply, quantity 1 set
  • G: cable tray and retaining clips, quantity 1 set
  • H: M6 x 16 mm frame bolts, quantity 8
  • J: M6 x 12 mm foot bolts, quantity 8
  • K: 4 x 16 mm tabletop and handset screws, quantity 10
Assemble the desk upside down on a protective blanket. In this fictional sample, the tabletop has factory-drilled pilot holes and all electrical connectors are keyed and labeled. If a part or hole position does not match this procedure, do not force the connection.
  1. Lay the desktop face down on the protective blanket with its rear edge facing you.
    ADDITIONAL INFORMATION: Identify the pilot holes for the frame and handset. Keep the desktop supported across its length to prevent corner damage.
  2. Attach both motorized lifting columns (B) to the adjustable crossbar (C).
    ADDITIONAL INFORMATION: Align the left and right mounting plates and install four M6 x 16 mm frame bolts (H) per column. Start every bolt by hand before tightening evenly with the hex key.
    STEP RESULT: The frame is square, the cables point toward the controller bracket, and all eight frame bolts are secure.
  3. Install one floor foot (D) on each lifting column using four M6 x 12 mm bolts (J) per foot.
    ADDITIONAL INFORMATION: Orient both feet in the same direction. Tighten the bolts evenly; do not overtighten or use a powered impact driver.
  4. Center the assembled frame over the desktop pilot holes and fasten it with eight 4 x 16 mm screws (K).
    ADDITIONAL INFORMATION: Use only the supplied short screws to avoid penetrating the top surface. Tighten by hand until the frame brackets sit flush.
  5. Attach the memory handset (E) under the preferred front corner using the remaining two screws (K).
    ADDITIONAL INFORMATION: Ensure that the controls remain easy to reach and that the handset cable does not cross a lifting-column joint.
  6. Fit the control unit (F) into the frame bracket and connect the keyed cables while the mains plug remains disconnected.
    1. Connect the left column motor lead to port M1 and the right column lead to port M2.
    2. Connect the handset lead to port HS and the power supply's low-voltage output to port DC IN.
    3. Route the cables through the tray (G) and secure slack with retaining clips.
    ADDITIONAL INFORMATION: Leave a service loop at each moving leg and confirm that no cable can be pinched at the maximum or minimum height.
  7. With a second adult, turn the desk upright, position it on a level floor, and check the movement clearance.
    ADDITIONAL INFORMATION: Keep fingers away from the feet and moving column joints. Confirm that the desk does not rock and that the power cable reaches the outlet without strain.
  8. Connect the power supply to a grounded outlet and initialize the height controller.
    ADDITIONAL INFORMATION: Press and hold the Down button until the desk reaches its lowest position. Continue holding for five seconds until the handset displays RST; release and press Down once more to complete initialization. If the display reports a fault, see the fault-code reference.
    STEP RESULT: The controller displays the current height and the desk responds to both arrow buttons.
  9. Test the full travel path and program a comfortable position using a memory preset.
    ADDITIONAL INFORMATION: Move the desk through an unobstructed range while observing both legs. At the desired height, hold the M button until the display flashes, then press preset 1, 2, or 3 to save that position.
    STEP RESULT: Movement is smooth, the saved preset returns the desk to the expected height, and no cables touch moving parts.
Result
The desk is assembled and commissioned when the frame is stable, the initialization has completed, and the up/down and preset controls operate correctly. Do not use the desk if a foot, fastener, cable, or motor housing is damaged.
AFTER COMPLETING THE TASK
Place accessories on the desktop and distribute their weight evenly. Observe the payload limit documented in the product overview and review the maintenance schedule before handover.
