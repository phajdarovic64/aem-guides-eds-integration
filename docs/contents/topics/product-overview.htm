Product overview

The NordForm Elevate Pro 160 is a fictional, dual-motor office desk designed for a single-user workstation. Its electric lifting columns support a smooth transition between sitting and standing positions. A three-position memory handset stores preferred heights.
The 1600 mm by 800 mm desktop provides space for a monitor, keyboard, pointing device, and everyday desk accessories. This demonstration guide is written as realistic product documentation; all specifications, warranty terms, and part numbers are illustrative rather than manufacturer-certified.

Key features

  • Dual-motor lifting system: synchronized height movement with soft start and stop.
  • Memory handset: three presets, a digital height display, and up/down controls.
  • Cable management: a rear cable tray and clips help keep power cables away from moving components.
  • Stable work surface: a laminate-coated birch-plywood desktop designed for indoor office use.
  • Collision sensing: movement stops when an unexpected obstruction is detected; this feature does not eliminate pinch-point risks.

Important safety information

Warning: Disconnect the desk from the wall outlet before assembling, cleaning, inspecting, or servicing electrical or moving components. Do not open the motor housings or power supply.
Caution: Do not exceed a distributed accessory payload of 70 kg, excluding the supplied desktop. Uneven or point-loaded weight may reduce stability and performance.
Warning: Keep hands, children, pets, chairs, storage units, and loose cables clear of the desk's movement path. Allow at least 50 mm clearance from nearby furniture throughout the full travel range.
For installers: two adults must lift and turn the assembled desk upright. Do not attempt to raise the desktop by pulling on either motorized leg.
For everyday users: position monitor cables and power strips so they cannot become trapped when the desk moves.

Ergonomic setup

  1. Adjust the desktop until your forearms are approximately horizontal while typing.
  2. Position the monitor with its upper edge near eye level and at a comfortable viewing distance.
  3. Alternate between seated and standing postures; avoid treating standing as a replacement for regular movement breaks.
  4. Save comfortable seated and standing heights using the memory handset.
